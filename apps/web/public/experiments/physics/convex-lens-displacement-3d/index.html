Convex lens: focal length by the displacement method

Fix the object and the screen a distance D apart (more than 4f). Slide the lens between them: a sharp image appears at two positions, a distance d apart. Then f = (D² − d²) / 4D. Nothing has to be measured from the lens itself.

The idea

With the object and the screen fixed a distance D apart, the lens formula has two solutions when D > 4f. In the first position (u = x, v = D − x) the image is large; in the second the object and image distances are exchanged (u = D − x, v = x) and the image is small. The two positions are a distance d = D − 2x apart.

  • From u + v = D and v − u = d: u = (D − d)/2, v = (D + d)/2
  • Lens formula: f = uv/(u + v) = (D² − d²) / 4D
  • Two sharp positions exist only if D > 4f; at D = 4f they merge into one
  • The two image sizes h₁ and h₂ satisfy h₁ h₂ = h² (object size h), since m₁ m₂ = 1
  • Advantage: D and d are measured between marks on the bench, so the position of the optical centre of the lens does not matter

Check your readings

    Precautions

    • Find the rough focal length first (focus a distant window), and keep D more than 4f.
    • The object, lens and screen must be at the same height, with their centres on the optical axis.
    • Do not move the object or the screen while finding the two lens positions for one value of D.
    • At each position move the lens a little each side of the sharpest image and take the middle.
    • Repeat for at least three values of D and take the mean of f.
