Soil analysis: texture, moisture, pH and water holding capacity

Soils from a river bank, a kitchen garden and a paddy field are tested in four ways. Compare them, and connect what you find with the plants that grow at each site.

The tests

  • Texture: sedimentation; % of each layer = layer height ÷ total height × 100
  • Sand ≥ 70% → sandy; clay ≥ 40% → clayey; a mixture → loamy
  • Moisture (%) = (W₁ − W₂) ÷ W₁ × 100 (fresh and oven-dry mass)
  • pH: universal indicator paper on the soil filtrate; below 7 acidic, above 7 alkaline
  • Water holding capacity (%) = water held ÷ mass of dry soil × 100

    Precautions

    • Collect soil from about 10 cm below the surface, removing stones, roots and litter.
    • Weigh the fresh sample immediately in a closed container so that it does not lose moisture.
    • Dry to constant mass, and cool in a desiccator before weighing.
    • Use distilled water for the pH test and read the paper immediately.
    • Allow time for all the water to drain before reading the cylinder.
