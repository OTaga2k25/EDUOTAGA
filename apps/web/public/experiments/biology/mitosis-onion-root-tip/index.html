Mitosis in onion root tip: stages and mitotic index

Prepare an acetocarmine squash of an onion root tip, find cells in prophase, metaphase, anaphase and telophase under the microscope, and calculate the mitotic index = dividing cells ÷ total cells × 100.

The stages

  • Interphase: round nucleus with a nucleolus; chromosomes not visible
  • Prophase: chromosomes condense into visible threads; nucleus still round
  • Metaphase: nuclear envelope gone; chromosomes line up at the equatorial plate
  • Anaphase: sister chromatids separate; V-shaped chromosomes move to the poles
  • Telophase: two nuclei form at the poles; a cell plate forms between them
  • Mitotic index = (P + M + A + T) ÷ total cells × 100

    Precautions

    • Use only the tip (1–2 cm) of a healthy, actively growing root: the meristem is where cells divide.
    • Do not overheat in HCl; warm gently.
    • Squash gently and evenly, without letting the coverslip slide, so that the cells spread in one layer.
    • Remove excess stain with blotting paper; avoid air bubbles under the coverslip.
    • Count cells in several fields chosen at random.
