DNA isolation: from spinach, peas or papaya

Break the cells open, strip away membranes and proteins, and make the DNA come out of solution as white threads that you can wind onto a glass rod. Choose how to do each step: every choice changes what you get.

    What each step does

    • Grinding: breaks the cell walls; salt neutralises DNA’s negative charge so it can clump
    • Detergent: dissolves the lipid membranes (cell and nuclear) to release DNA
    • Filtering: removes cell debris
    • Protease (meat tenderiser, pineapple juice): digests the histone proteins bound to DNA
    • Ice-cold ethanol: DNA is insoluble in alcohol and precipitates at the boundary as white threads

      Precautions

      • Keep the ethanol in a freezer or on ice until you use it.
      • Stir gently after adding detergent; never blend or shake hard, or the DNA breaks.
      • Pour the ethanol slowly down the side of the tilted tube so that it forms a separate layer.
      • Do not disturb the tube while the DNA precipitates.
      • Ethanol is flammable: keep it away from flames.
