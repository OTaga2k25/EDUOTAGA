Mendelian inheritance: monohybrid 3 : 1 and dihybrid 9 : 3 : 3 : 1

Beads in two bags stand for the gametes of two heterozygous pea plants. Drawing one bead from each bag at random is like fertilisation. Count enough zygotes and Mendel’s ratios appear by chance alone.

What seed does the zygote give?

The idea

  • Each parent Rr makes gametes R and r in equal numbers (law of segregation)
  • Rr × Rr → genotypes RR : Rr : rr = 1 : 2 : 1; phenotypes round : wrinkled = 3 : 1
  • RrYy × RrYy → 9 round yellow : 3 round green : 3 wrinkled yellow : 1 wrinkled green
  • The two characters segregate independently (law of independent assortment)
  • Return each bead after drawing so that the bags always hold equal proportions

    Precautions

    • Use beads of the same size and texture so that they cannot be told apart by touch.
    • Draw without looking, and shake the bag before each draw.
    • Return each bead to its bag after recording, to keep the proportions equal.
    • Take a large number of draws: small samples can differ a lot from the expected ratio by chance.
